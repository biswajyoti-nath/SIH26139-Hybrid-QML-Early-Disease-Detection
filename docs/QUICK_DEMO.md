## Script

**(0:00 - 0:10)**  
"This is our evidence-gated hybrid QML platform."

**(0:10 - 0:30)**  
"Rather than assuming quantum is better, we first profile the biomedical data. As you can see on the dashboard, we extract key complexity metrics like variance retained and linear separability."

**(0:30 - 0:50)**  
"Then we evaluate classical and multiple quantum pathways under the exact same protocol to ensure a fair comparison."

**(0:50 - 1:10)**  
"Here is our WDBC experiment evidence. The classical SVM reaches 0.995 ROC-AUC, while QSVM reaches 0.842 and VQC 0.665."

**(1:10 - 1:30)**  
"Notice the runtime difference. Quantum simulation is also much more computationally expensive."

**(1:30 - 1:50)**  
"When we click 'Evaluate Pathways', the router processes this evidence and recommends 'CLASSICAL PREFERRED'."

**(1:50 - 2:00)**  
"That is intentional. Our system does not force quantum. It actively measures whether the quantum pathway is justified, falling back to classical methods when quantum paths are bottlenecked. This ensures patient safety is uncompromised while we search for quantum advantage."
